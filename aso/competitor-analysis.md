# Competitor Analysis — Peptide / GLP-1 / TRT Tracker Niche

US App Store, researched June 2026. The dedicated peptide-tracker category has exploded into a crowded,
fragmented field. Naming is so generic ("Peptide Tracker," "Peptide Calculator," "PepCalc," "Pep") that
title/keyword collisions are rampant and brand recall is weak for most players. That is Stackr's opening: a
single sharp brand that pairs a best-in-class reconstitution calculator with full tracking, generous free
use, and visible social proof.

## The three lanes

1. **Pure calculators** — PepCalc, Peptides Calculator. No ongoing tracking; lose any user who wants protocol management.
2. **Full peptide+TRT protocol trackers** — Regimen, Dosed, Pep AI, the two "Peptide Tracker & Calculator" listings.
3. **GLP-1 / weight-loss-focused trackers** — GLP-1 Tracker: Pep, Shotsy, Glapp (scoped narrower than Stackr).

## Direct competitors

### Regimen: Peptide Tracker — **the one to beat**
- **Title/subtitle:** "Regimen: Peptide Tracker" — *Track peptides, GLP-1s, TRT, and 150+ compounds, and connect every dose to your health metrics.*
- **Rating:** 4.9 ★, 230+ ratings (claims #1 / highest-rated). By Awaken Labs LLC.
- **Targets:** peptide tracker, #1 peptide tracker, GLP-1, TRT, 150–600+ compound library, half-life curves, lab tracking, built-in calculator suite, BPC-157, TB-500, CJC-1295, Ipamorelin, stacks.
- **Gap Stackr can exploit:** free tier limited to ONE compound; full power behind subscription; broad/complex feature set feels heavy for users who mainly want fast reconstitution math. **Attack on:** simplicity, calculator-first speed, generous free use, price — and own the `reconstitution calculator` niche Regimen treats as one of many features.

### Peptide Tracker Calculator (PeptideCalc.io) — strongest calculator hybrid
- **Subtitle:** *peptide and GLP-1 calculator for reconstitution, syringe units, schedules, reminders, dose logs, and inventory tracking.*
- **Rating:** 4.89 ★, ~170. By Wild Monkey Labs. **$4.99 one-time, no subscription / no ads.**
- **Targets:** peptide tracker, peptide calculator, reconstitution calculator, GLP-1, TRT, steroids, syringe units, dose log, inventory, premixed vials, Lipo-C/Lipo-B, injector pens, BAC water, sterile water.
- **Gap:** the $4.99 paywall means **no free funnel**; inconsistent store naming hurts keywords; calculator-first identity is thin on outcome correlation/analytics. **Attack on:** free entry, progress insights, a cleaner single brand.

### GLP-1 Tracker: Pep — biggest GLP-1-only player
- **Subtitle:** *Your entire GLP-1 routine together — injections or pills, reminders, progress tracking, nutrition insights.*
- **Rating:** 4.69 ★, ~450 ratings, 11K+ downloads. By Shred Apps / MWM.
- **Targets:** GLP-1 tracker, Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound, Rybelsus, compounded sema/tirz, injection site rotation, estimated med levels, weight, progress photos, macros.
- **Gap:** **scoped to GLP-1 weight loss only** — no research-peptide library, no reconstitution calculator, not a fit for TRT/peptide-stack users. **Attack on:** the broader peptide/TRT/reconstitution audience Pep ignores, while still covering GLP-1 dosing.

### Pep AI: Peptide GLP-1 Tracker
- **Subtitle:** *All-in-one peptide & GLP-1 tracking with AI insights for inventory, logs, nutrition and wellness.*

### Dosed — Peptide Tracker & Log

## Competitive takeaways for Stackr

1. **Social proof is the real moat.** Regimen (4.9/230+) and PepTracker (4.7/361) win on reviews. Stackr's
   single highest-ROI growth lever is **driving rating volume** — it influences both rank and conversion.
2. **Own the calculator niche, but as a tracker.** Calculator-only apps (PepCalc, Peptides Calculator) and a
   wall of free web tools make calculator-only positioning weak. Stackr's edge is *calculator + full tracking
   + generous free tier* in one clean brand.
3. **Compounds are under-owned.** Rivals *list* BPC-157/TB-500/retatrutide but almost none *rank* on the exact
   terms. That's where the cheap wins are (see `keyword-research.md`).
4. **Drug brand names are a trap.** Dosed and Pep stuff Ozempic/Wegovy/Mounjaro into metadata — that's against
   Apple's trademark rules. Don't copy it; use Apple Search Ads for those terms instead.
